The Whitney Experience

Where brownstone elegance meets modern luxury.

The Whitney Hotel is a refined gem among Boston's boutique hotels, welcoming guests to a calming retreat where uncomplicated luxury resides. Inspired by American Industrialist Henry Melville Whitney, every detail — from lush gardens by Matthew Cunningham to interiors by Ealain Studio — is thoughtfully crafted.

Blending seamlessly with Beacon Hill's black-shuttered townhouses and fashionable boutiques, this luxury boutique hotel in Boston is an unexpected find where guests are indulged and instantly at home. Recognized among Boston's top hotels by Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice Awards 2025.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

The Whitney Hotel is located at 170 Charles Street, Boston, MA 02114, in the heart of the historic Beacon Hill neighborhood. It's just steps from the Charles River, Boston Common, Public Garden, and the Massachusetts General Hospital.

Room rates at The Whitney Hotel Boston start from $409 per night for a Classic King Guestroom. Beacon Hill City View rooms start at $499, Charles River Corner Kings from $599, and Premium King Suites from $899. Prices vary by season and availability — check today's rates.

Yes! The Whitney Hotel Boston welcomes dogs. The hotel is fully pet-friendly, making it a great choice for travelers bringing their four-legged companions to explore Beacon Hill.

The Whitney Hotel Boston offers free high-speed WiFi, the on-site Peregrine restaurant (coastal Mediterranean cuisine), a state-of-the-art fitness studio, complimentary bikes for exploring Beacon Hill, 24-hour in-room dining, turndown service, Frette bathrobes, organic Grown Alchemist bath amenities, a private hidden garden, and full ADA accessibility.

The Whitney Hotel Boston has received the MICHELIN Guide Two Keys award, AAA Four Diamond rating, Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice 2025, and LEED Certification. It's consistently ranked among the top boutique hotels in Boston.
